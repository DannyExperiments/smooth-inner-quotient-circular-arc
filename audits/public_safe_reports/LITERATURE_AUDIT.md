# Q1346 literature audit

Primary statement: [Fricain–Grivaux–Ostermann Question 8.19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.03303v3#page=64).

Douglas–Rudin proved uniform approximation by quotients of inner functions. Here the quotient represents the prescribed boundary data exactly. The smooth density gives a rapidly decreasing remainder and controls all endpoint derivatives.

Primary prior work: [original source](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1969.31.313).

Endpoint values are one-sided at the Cayley cut. The result establishes the quotient construction and does not determine the hypercyclicity of a Toeplitz operator.

Fresh search on 2026-11-27 found no exact prior answer. Confidence is high in the source and scope match, moderate in this bounded absence-of-collision result.
