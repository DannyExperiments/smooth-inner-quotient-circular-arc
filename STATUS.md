The argument function π + 2 arctan(t) is strictly increasing with derivative 2/(1+t²). For every positive A and prescribed length strictly between zero and 2π, the proof gives a positive scale realizing that length with positive endpoint angular speed. Lean also proves that exponentiating this phase gives the exact Möbius quotient (t−i)/(t+i).
