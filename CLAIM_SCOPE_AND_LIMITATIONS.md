# Claim scope and limitations

Fricain, Grivaux and Ostermann’s Question 8.19 has an affirmative answer. The boundary quotient of two inner functions admits a smooth injective parametrization on the full closed angular interval, with nonvanishing derivative at both endpoints. Any nondegenerate proper closed arc can be obtained in either orientation.

Endpoint values are one-sided at the Cayley cut. The result establishes the quotient construction and does not determine the hypercyclicity of a Toeplitz operator.

The literature search is bounded. External peer review is pending.

The argument function π + 2 arctan(t) is strictly increasing with derivative 2/(1+t²). For every positive A and prescribed length strictly between zero and 2π, the proof gives a positive scale realizing that length with positive endpoint angular speed. Lean also proves that exponentiating this phase gives the exact Möbius quotient (t−i)/(t+i). The real kernel estimates, smooth extension at the Cayley cut and common-outer-factor representation by inner functions are in the manuscript. [Lean coverage](formal/closing/README.md)
